# Win Huson 2

The minimalist, space-saving design features a modern "notch" and blur effects, providing a spacious workspace while ensuring easy access to menus—including quick launch, the taskbar, time and calendar, weather (with conditions and temperature), video playback visuals, and audio controls. Additionally, you can quickly access and pin system tray items for easy management and utilize a quick-access control center. You also have the option to disable the notch for a seamless main bar display.

## Widgets

- Home
- Quick Launch
- Taskbar
- Active Window
- Clock
- Custom
- Open Meteo
- Audio Visualizer
- Media
- Systray
- Battery
- WiFi
- Volume
- Notifications
- Control Center

## Requirements

- JetBrainsMono NFP
