# Sequoia Menu Bar

Sequoia Menu Bar brings the macOS Sequoia menu bar to YASB on Windows.
Transparent top bar with a soft vertical shade, Apple menu, active window title (truncated to 40 characters) and File / View / Go / Help menus.
A notch-style center island with the clock and a Pomodoro timer. Click to open the timer, middle-click resets it, right-click starts or pauses.
Status items: systray, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, volume, battery and Control Center.
Control Center uses liquid-glass tiles and cards: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Focus, Dark Mode, Mute and Mic, plus brightness / volume / microphone sliders and a Now Playing card.
Calendar popup with holidays and alarms/timers; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth panels are styled to match.
All panels are transparent glass with a soft shadow and 12-20px corners.
Looks best on a dark or photo wallpaper. Text is white; the vertical shade keeps it readable.

## Widgets

- Home
- Active Window
- Clock
- Pomodoro
- Systray
- Bluetooth
- WiFi
- Volume
- Battery
- Control Center

## Requirements

- JetBrainsMono Nerd Font (https://www.nerdfonts.com/font-downloads)
- SF Pro Text and SF Symbols 6 (optional, for the Apple logo and menu typography)
