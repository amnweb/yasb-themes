# Rangalipi

Rangalipi is drawn from Indian folk art — a slim 32 px rail across the top of the screen, with hand-drawn mandala motifs fading in behind every popup at low opacity, so the artwork shows through without hurting readability.

The left side carries the komorebi workspaces, layout and stack, the centre has the clock with its Indian holiday calendar, cava and media, and the right side groups the tray with volume, battery, wallpapers and the control centre. Every motif loads from the Rangalipi artwork repository over URLs, so there is nothing extra to install — apply the config and the bar is ready.
